About the Student Information Portal

Portal Purpose & Overview

The University Student Information Portal is a centralized digital management system designed to streamline academic administration, track student enrolment, and facilitate transparent communication between students, faculty, and administrators. The portal enables prospective and continuing students to register their details, verify their admitted academic programmes, monitor years of study, and maintain accurate institutional records seamlessly.
